# CareGrid — Pitch Deck

**Track:** The Next Big Decacorn · Paris Builds (Y Combinator × Unaite)

> *"Doctolib helps you book an appointment that exists. CareGrid creates appointments that didn't exist yesterday."*

---

### 1 · Cover
**CareGrid — the capacity layer for healthcare.** AI that assembles idle rooms, free practitioners and waiting patients into brand-new medical sessions.

### 2 · Problem
Healthcare doesn't only have a *shortage* problem — it has a **coordination and utilization** problem: clinics sit at ~60% capacity while patients wait weeks.

### 3 · The hidden waste
Every weekend, equipped rooms sit empty, specialists are available in fragments, and 187 patients wait — yet nothing assembles these pieces into care.

### 4 · Insight
The supply already exists; it's just **unmatched in time and space**. The bottleneck isn't beds or doctors — it's the orchestration nobody owns.

### 5 · Solution
CareGrid is an AI agent that *creates* capacity: it matches rooms × practitioners × staff × equipment × patient demand × urgency × no-show risk into one optimized, ready-to-run session.

### 6 · Product — patient-first, with an engine behind it
**Patients** search by specialty + location and get booked into brand-new capacity near them, weeks earlier · **Clinics** watch the engine assemble idle rooms + doctors + waitlist into packed sessions · **Doctors** accept turnkey offers in one tap.

### 7 · The key insight — doctors aren't tied to clinics
A specialist free on Saturday and an equipped room sitting empty in a *different* clinic are a match nobody makes today. CareGrid treats doctors as independent, matches them into any clinic with the right equipment, and builds travel time into the plan.

### 8 · Demo
A patient who'd otherwise wait weeks for dermatology searches → CareGrid offers a brand-new slot available within days, 1.6 km away. Behind it: one "Detect" composes **17 sessions · 202 patients · +€33,592**, assembling **12 doctors (5 independent, 5 visiting)** into idle evening & weekend rooms — equipment respected, travel built in, every day packed slot-by-slot.

### 9 · Why this is real AI (not a filter)
A reasoning engine ingests the full state — multi-clinic rooms + equipment, an independent doctor network with availabilities, and a waitlist by specialty/urgency/geography — and assembles optimized, packed sessions with travel and equipment constraints. Orchestration, not search.

### 10 · Why now
Post-pandemic backlogs across Europe, chronic staffing shortages, normalized flexible/locum work, clinics under margin pressure — and AI agents finally good enough to optimize multi-constraint scheduling.

### 11 · Market
~150k+ outpatient clinics in Europe. CareGrid unlocks ~€150k/yr of net-new revenue per clinic → **€2.7B SAM in EU outpatient**, $10B+ globally as the capacity layer for all elective care.

### 12 · Business model
We monetize **value created, not seats**: a 10–15% take rate on net-new revenue we generate, plus an ops SaaS tier. Practitioners pay nothing — they earn. Patients can subscribe to "find care, sooner."

### 13 · Moat
A compounding matching graph: every session improves no-show prediction and patient–capacity matching. Three-sided network effects — more practitioners → more sessions → more patients → more clinics.

### 14 · Vision
CareGrid becomes the **utilization operating system** for healthcare: the layer that turns the world's idle medical capacity into care that reaches patients in days, not months.

### 15 · Ask
Own the capacity layer for a multi-trillion-euro system running at 60%. That's the decacorn.
